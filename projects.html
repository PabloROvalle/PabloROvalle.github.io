JWST ERS

ERS 1373

PI Imke de Pater and Thierry Fouchet

Analysis of the Jovian system from the first JWST data. I worked on the data reduction and cleaning of MIRI, NIRSpec and NIRCam observations for Jupiter and its moons, and I led the MIRI/MRS analysis of the south polar region to study the impact of auroral precipitation on the atmosphere.

ANR

ANR PRESSE

PI Thierry Fouchet

I was part of this ANR project, which aimed to analyse the ERS 1373 data set and prepare the French science community for future JWST observations of the Solar System.

Jupiter exogenic molecules

PI Pablo Rodríguez Ovalle
